URGENT ISSUES

Recognize when to ask for help.

01

Burst pipes

Uncontrolled water from a damaged pipe.

02

Major leaks

Persistent water flow or rapidly spreading moisture.

03

Blocked drains

Significant backup or loss of drainage.

04

Overflowing fixtures

Water escaping a toilet, sink or other fixture.

05

Water supply problems

An unexpected loss of supply or sudden system changes.

06

Water heater leaks

Water pooling around the tank or leaking from connected pipework.

BEFORE HELP ARRIVES

A few calm steps. A safer space.

Protect people first, then share what you can see with your plumber.

  • 01

    Keep clear of hazards

    Avoid wet electrical equipment, contaminated water and unstable surfaces.

  • 02

    Limit water only if safe

    Use a known, accessible shutoff. Leave unfamiliar valves and repairs to a professional.

  • 03

    Make room for help

    Keep people away from the affected area and clear a safe route for the technician.

Immediate danger? Contact the appropriate local emergency service. This form is not monitored for emergencies.
