BEFORE YOU APPROVE

Good questions lead to clear expectations.

01Are these actual prices?

No. All displayed prices are illustrative demo content and are not market quotes.

02What should an estimate include?

Ask for the scope, labor, materials, taxes, call-out fees, possible additional charges and any conditions.

03What happens if the scope changes?

The provider should explain the change and obtain your approval before proceeding with additional work.

KNOW WHAT YOU ARE AGREEING TO

A clear estimate.
A confident next step.

Good decisions start with a clear conversation. Ask for an explanation of the proposed work and keep a written record of what is included.

01

A defined scope

The issue being addressed, the work proposed and any exclusions.

02

Costs explained

Labour, materials and any assessment, travel or additional charges.

03

A plan for changes

How unexpected findings, extra work and timing will be discussed.
